About the model

What the prediction and the risk mean

Model
Linear regression in scikit-learn, packed into the dashboard image
Inputs
Total intensity (veh/hr) and hour of day (UTC)
Risk
A sigmoid around 40 µg/m³ with steepness 0.2, so the risk is 50% when the prediction is exactly 40
Levels
Low under 20%  Moderate 20–50%  High 50% or more
Caveat
40 µg/m³ is the EU annual mean limit. Here it is applied to single hours as an early warning.

NDW publishes traffic for the whole country about every minute. The traffic ingest keeps the four A27 sites, adds them up per hour and writes one CSV per site to S3.
